# taghizadeh2024mfgnn — Multifidelity GNNs for Efficient and Accurate Mesh-Based PDEs

**Authors**: Taghizadeh et al. CACAIE 2025 (online 2024).
**Link**: [DOI](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mice.13312)

## Why this paper matters (limited)

GNN backbone with multi-fidelity input fusion. Designed for unstructured-mesh PDE problems. Less applicable to regular-grid `ifc_*` data than FNO.

- **Not used** as a primary inspiration this cycle.
- Candidate for future cycles if/when the factory tackles irregular-mesh datasets.
