fno_mf_stack: 4 small FNOs (~50k params each, hidden=32-64) — one per fidelity level. MFRNP-style residual stacking with decoder-in-the-aggregation. Final HF output = aggregate(decoded_LFs upsampled to 64x64) + delta where delta is the HF FNO prediction. Per-fidelity output normalization. val_frac=0.1. Inspired by niu2024mfrnp and li2020fno. Target: ifc_poisson 18.5 -> <=0.5; ifc_heat 0.149 -> <=0.10.
