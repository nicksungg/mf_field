### Anti-patterns to Avoid (Cycle-010)

- **NK1 — Pure m-conditioning on HF-only FNO (cycle-008 H2 REVERT).** FiLM-via-LayerNorm with affines `γ(m, m²)` on a single HF FNO cannot synthesize the LF→HF correlation pathway that `ifc_poisson` requires. Evidence: `fno_coreg_conditioned` cycle-008 H2 (`experiment/12 @ 540e684`): heat 0.017102 (competitive rank-2) BUT poisson 0.749915 = 12.6× over `fno_mf_stack` Poisson; composite regressed +9.66% vs banked best. **Cycle-010 application:** neither H1 (in-family capacity-only on existing MFRNP residual stack, no conditioning change) nor H2 (curriculum on existing arch, no conditioning change) engages this axis. NK1-clear for both.

- **NK2 — Frozen-LF curricula on co-evolved residual ladders (cycle-008 H3 REVERT) — WITH PUBLISHED CARVE-OUT FOR H2.** Multi-stage curricula that freeze the LF stack mid-training are INCOMPATIBLE with co-evolved residual ladder designs (`fno_coregionalization`, `fno_coreg_residual`). Evidence: cycle-008 H3 (`fno_coreg_residual` three-stage, `experiment/13 @ 420a51c`) — `fno_coreg_residual × heat` regressed 19× (0.026277 → 0.509105), universal `ifc_heat > 0.0194` kill-switch TRIPPED 26× over threshold; `fno_coreg_residual × poisson` regressed 2.15×.
  - **H2 carve-out (load-bearing):** `fno_mf_stack`'s structural difference is the explicit NK2 escape clause — `models/fno_mf_stack/model.py:165-168` constructs 4 **independent** `SmallFNO` modules with NO shared weights, coupled only through a 1×1-conv `MFRNPAggregator` MLP on the output (research.md §O2 "NK2 carve-out analysis (load-bearing)"; failure_analysis §"Recommended Interventions > 2" "Carve-out justification vs NK2"). The NK2 anti-pattern's mechanism (shared K-basis losing joint gradient signal when LF is frozen) does NOT apply here — the LF stack is loss-supervised in stage 1 and gradient-isolated in stage 2 with no shared parameters. Pattern matches `yang2025mfdeeponet`'s freeze-branch-and-trunk + train-only-merge protocol precisely.
  - **MANDATORY guardrails for H2 (per the carve-out's price):** dual kill-switch (absolute Poisson/Heat ≤ 0.0594 AND inter-stage stage-2_best_val ≥ 0.90 × stage1_best_val); wall ≤ 1800s. H2's PR MUST declare all four kill-switch trip conditions in its experiment manifest. H1 sidesteps NK2 entirely (no curriculum).

- **NK3 — MFRNP-style loss-weight transfer to coregionalization family (3/3 REVERT, cycles 003/006/007).** Per-dataset MFRNP loss-weight tuning (HF=2.0, LF=0.25 and variants) on the coregionalization family produces heat-invariance violations even when the heat branch is claimed invariant by construction. Evidence: cycle-003 H1 (Poisson5 transplant `fno_mf_stack → fno_coreg_residual`: poisson +268% regression — REVERT); cycle-006 H1 (reverse cross-architecture transplant: REVERT); cycle-007 H2 (within-family per-dataset dispatch on `fno_coreg_residual`: heat 0.02628 → 0.03487 +32.7% at 6.5× the noise band — REVERT). **Cycle-010 application:** H1 holds `poisson_hf_weight=2.0, poisson_lf_weight=0.25` UNCHANGED on `fno_mf_stack` (canonical MFRNP-native recipe for this family; NOT subject to the NK3 cross-family transfer pattern). H2 is loss-weight-agnostic across stages (canonical weights unchanged; staging only). Both NK3-clear.
