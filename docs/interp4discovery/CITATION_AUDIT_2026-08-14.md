## Method

No bibliographic field in `references.bib` comes from model memory.
Every field was fetched from one of four sources and cross-checked:

1. **arXiv Atom API** — title, full author list, submission date, `journal_ref`, DOI, primary category. 77 IDs fetched; all 77 resolved.
2. **Crossref REST** `/works/{doi}` — title, container, volume/issue/pages, authors, publisher. 36 DOIs.
3. **DBLP publication search** and **Semantic Scholar** `/paper/batch` and **OpenAlex** — peer-reviewed venue for papers whose arXiv record has no `journal_ref`.
4. **Repo-internal provenance files** — transcribed, not re-searched, per the handoff: `papers_summary.csv`, `datasets_summary.csv`, `baselines/paper_baselines.json`, `SURF_2026-main/PDE_SOLVERS.md`, and the per-dataset `meta.json` `source` fields.

### The venue rule

A venue is written into the `.bib` only when it is backed by an authoritative single source (DBLP, Crossref, or the arXiv `journal_ref`), or by two independent indexes agreeing, or by one index agreeing with the venue asserted in the registry.
Otherwise the entry is emitted as an arXiv preprint and the unconfirmed hint is recorded in the TSV — never silently promoted into the bibliography.
49 of 67 arXiv-sourced entries cleared that bar; the other 18 are listed below.

### Checks the sources cannot express, asserted caller-side

- every declared key resolves to a fetched record (no key invented);
- the surname in each key matches the record's actual first author — this caught `mffm2026`, whose first author is Sipeng Chen, now `chen2026mffm`;
- the year in each key matches the emitted year — this caught five keys carrying the arXiv year instead of the publication year (`fernandezgodino`, `george`, `ren`, `villatoro`, `cunningham`);
- no arXiv ID used twice, no duplicate keys;
- every hand override carries a written reason.

## Findings

### 1. A fabricated citation was in the tree, including in the winning model — fixed

`beggs2025pdecond` does not exist.
Both BibTeX blocks carrying it invented an author ("Beggs, and others" / "Beggs, et al.") and a title ("FiLM-Conditioned Neural Operators for PDE-Parameter Generalization" / "FiLM-via-LayerNorm Conditioning for Parametric PDE Surrogates"), while pointing at a real arXiv ID, 2509.09599.
That ID is **Shokar, Kerswell & Haynes, "Conditioning on PDE Parameters to Generalise Deep Learning Emulation of Stochastic and Chaotic Dynamics."**

It appeared in 12 places across 5 model families, including `mf_fno_transfer_film` — the paper's headline model.

The mechanism attribution was also wrong, so it could not simply be re-pointed.
I fetched the Shokar abstract: it conditions on PDE parameters, pre-trains then fine-tunes, and uses **local attention**.
It contains no FiLM and no LayerNorm-affine modulation.
So the fix splits the claim:

- the FiLM-via-norm *mechanism* now cites `dumoulin2018featurewise` (Distill 2018), which is what actually establishes conditional/adaptive normalisation;
- the PDE-parameter-conditioning *precedent* now cites `shokar2025pdecond`, with the distinction stated inline in `fno_coreg_conditioned/INSPIRATION.md`.

Files changed (all under `models/**`, the modifiable surface):
`dg_fno/{INSPIRATION.md,model.py,manifest.json}`, `fno_coreg_conditioned/{INSPIRATION.md,model.py}`, `mf_fno_transfer_film/{model.py,manifest.json}`, `mf_fno_pinn_transfer/model.py`, `mf_fno_transfer_pinn/model.py`.
Zero occurrences of `beggs` remain under `models/`.

**Consequence to note:** four `model.py` files changed, so `eval/score.py`'s `code_hash` for those families changes and their cached smoke results invalidate.
The edits are comment/docstring-only — no behaviour change — and none of the affected caches feed `final_error_matrix.csv`.
The same fabricated key also appears in `.claude/worktrees/vivid-tinkering-sifakis/` and in `.factory/archive/` history, which I left alone (archive of what happened, and a worktree I do not own).

### 5. Two machine-metadata defects, corrected with written reasons

- Crossref's record for Kennedy & O'Hagan (`10.1093/biomet/87.1.1`) lists **only Kennedy**. Author list completed by hand.

Publisher-side cosmetic damage is repaired generically in the generator: HTML italics stripped from titles (Rayleigh 1916), leading roman-numeral article numbers removed, all-caps titles and surnames title-cased (Fisher 1937).
