## A. The film model: exact architecture + training recipe as implemented

Source files: `mf_field/factory_mffp/models/mf_fno_transfer_film/{model.py, smoke_eval.py, manifest.json}`.
Note: the family directory contains NO `INSPIRATION.md` (only `manifest.json`, `model.py`, `smoke_eval.py`); citations live in `manifest.json`'s `inspired_by` list: `li2020fno`, `perez2018film`, `beggs2025pdecond`, `lyu2023mffno` — and `beggs2025pdecond` is a known-wrong citation key (the authors are Shokar, Kerswell & Haynes, arXiv:2509.09599; flagged in `paper/RELATED_WORK_RESEARCH.md` and the paper-draft TODO list).

### Backbone (model.py)

- Task signature: condition vector `X ∈ ℝ^cond_dim → field (B, H, W)` on a fixed working grid; the network INPUT is only the 2 coordinate channels (a `linspace(-1,1)` meshgrid buffer); X never appears as an input channel (`FNO2d.forward`, model.py:153-160).
- Structure: `lift` = Conv2d(2→64, 1×1) → 4 × `FNOBlock` → `proj` = Conv2d(64→64,1×1)+GELU+Conv2d(64→1,1×1).
- Each `FNOBlock` (model.py:103-119): `GELU(FiLMNorm(SpectralConv2d(z) + Conv2d1x1(z), X))`.
- `SpectralConv2d` (model.py:44-69): rfft2 (ortho), keep `min(modes_cap, H//2)` × `min(modes_cap, W//2+1)` modes with two complex weight tensors (positive and negative H-frequencies), irfft2 back; documented as byte-for-byte identical to the concat baseline `mf_fno_transfer_bar/model.py` so only conditioning differs.
- FiLM conditioning site (model.py:72-100): `FiLMNorm` = affine-free `GroupNorm(min(8, C), C)` followed by `γ(X)·ẑ + β(X)`, where `[γ;β] = MLP(X)` with hidden width 64 (`cond_dim → 64 → GELU → 2·channels`); the last Linear is zero-initialized (weights and bias) and `γ = 1 + γ_raw`, so the network starts as an unconditioned FNO and learns to modulate.
- FiLM conditions on X ONLY; the fidelity index m never enters the network — multi-fidelity behavior lives entirely in the training schedule (model.py docstring lines 23-27; contrast `fno_coreg_conditioned`, which FiLMs on m while concatenating X).

### Hyperparameters and training recipe (smoke_eval.py)

- `SMOKE` dict (smoke_eval.py:51-52): hidden_channels=64, n_blocks=4, modes_cap=12, batch_size=16, lr_pretrain=1e-3, lr_finetune=3e-4, weight_decay=1e-5, grad_clip=1.0.
- Working grid: HF native grid capped at 256 on the longer side with aspect preserved (`WORK_CAP=256`, `_cap_grid`); modes = `(min(12, H//2), min(12, W//2+1))`.
- Transfer/warm-start scheme (smoke_eval.py:163-173): (1) pretrain the single FNO on the abundant LF split (fields resampled to the working grid) for `--epochs` at lr 1e-3, supervised in `y/scaler_lf` space; (2) fine-tune the SAME weights on the scarce HF split for `--epochs` at lr 3e-4 in `y/scaler_hf` space; (3) evaluate on the HF test split, de-normalizing by `scaler_hf`.
- Per-stage output scaler = `max|y|` over that stage's training fields (smoke_eval.py:137-138).
- Optimizer: AdamW + CosineAnnealingLR (eta_min 1e-6), MSE loss, grad-norm clip 1.0, fixed-seed permutation generator.
- Resampling: `_to_grid` routes through `models/_common/lf_registration.resample_fields`, applying a per-dataset registration convention (node-aligned for the sharp panel, Dirichlet interior-node for helmholtz, historic cell-centred bilinear elsewhere — the 2026-08-01 registration-defect fix, ADR r2-0001).
- Checkpoint resume from `ckpt_dir/last.pt` keyed on `(epochs_target, grid)` (mandatory for preemptible SLURM).
- Parameter count ~4.7M at default width (paper draft §5.5); param delta vs concat baseline is only the 4 per-block FiLM MLPs.
- Result JSON records `mf_mechanism: transfer_learning_pretrain_LF_finetune_HF` and `conditioning: film_on_X_per_block`.

### Two structural facts an interpretability paper should lead with

- The model never consumes the LF FIELD at inference — it is a pure `X → field` regression; the LF data acts only through weight initialization (verified against code by `docs/reports/MF_FNO_CNN_Hybrid_Report.md` §2 and `docs/reports/MF_Sharp_HighFreq_Report.md` §0.4).
- All sample-to-sample variation must flow through 8 numbers per channel per block (γ, β): the conditioning bottleneck is 4 blocks × 2×64 modulation parameters generated from X.

## C. Existing mechanistic/interpretive claims about WHY film wins

Labeled VERIFIED (a measurement exists behind it) vs HYPOTHESIS (stated/argued but never measured).

1. **Mechanism not capacity** — VERIFIED. The param-matched concat control (`mf_fno_transfer_2m`, 0.01246) fails to close the gap to film (0.00815) with an identical backbone and schedule (paper_draft.md §5.2; leaderboard_table.csv rows 2-4).
2. **Film wins BOTH smooth and hard regimes**, removing the prior "fusion overtakes on hard data" caveat — VERIFIED (measured regime split, paper_draft.md §5.3 table: film 0.00176/0.17545 vs concat 0.00289/0.22262 vs coreg_residual 0.00458/0.20766).
3. **"A constant channel is pure k=0, so the spectral convolution barely transforms the concatenated condition, and it enters only once at the bottom; FiLM re-injects X at every block"** — HYPOTHESIS. This is the paper's central causal story (paper_draft.md §3, abstract; model.py docstring lines 9-21) but no spectral/probing measurement of the trained networks backs it anywhere in these documents.
4. **"Global parameter vector → global per-channel modulation is the natural inductive match; nothing is lost dropping the spatial encoding"** — HYPOTHESIS (paper_draft.md §3, §7; model.py docstring).
5. **FiLM's edge should widen under HF scarcity (few-shot)** — HYPOTHESIS, explicitly flagged "not yet run" (paper_draft.md §5.4 and Appendix A TODO).
6. **FiLM buys per-dataset robustness (Elo breadth), not lower typical error** — VERIFIED in the 30-family zoo view (median 0.0154 vs concat 0.0151 tie, Elo #1 vs #8; MF_SOTA_vs_MFFP_Report §3.1-3.2) — but note the 15-dataset geomean view shows a real 1.5× error improvement, so which framing holds depends on the aggregate.
7. **The transfer winner never consumes the LF field at inference; MF lives entirely in the pretraining schedule** — VERIFIED by code audit (smoke_eval.py; MF_FNO_CNN_Hybrid_Report §2; MF_Sharp_HighFreq_Report §0.4).
8. **The 12-mode spectral wall floors film (and all FNO families) on sharp fields at ~3–10% rel-L2; misalignment makes additive residuals dipoles; MSE+rel-L2 structurally prefer blur** — HYPOTHESIS/analytic: derived from analytic prototypes ("None of them require running a single experiment", MF_Sharp_HighFreq_Report §0), never measured on the real datasets (MF_FNO_CNN_Hybrid_Report §4.1 says so explicitly).
9. **On sharp data film survives only if X pins the sharp-feature position** — HYPOTHESIS (MF_Sharp_HighFreq_Report §0.4 archetype table: "degrades; stays best-in-zoo if X is informative").
10. **Why film loses its 5 benchmark_30 datasets / wins the other 24** — explicitly LEFT AS LABELED HYPOTHESES: "why each side wins where it does is left as labeled hypotheses until the round-3 diagnostic probes are run on these residuals" (PROFESSOR_UPDATE §2 caveats; benchmark30 report.md "Interpretation").
11. **"Film's lighter conditioning suffices where LF is already informative; r3s2's routing/stacking machinery adds error there"** — HYPOTHESIS, flagged as such ("a hypothesis, not established by these artifacts", benchmark30 report.md "Where r3s2 loses hardest").
12. **Film's ext__helmholtz_2d instability (rel-L2 4.43, worse than predicting zero)** — VERIFIED measurement, unexplained mechanism (benchmark30 report.md).
13. **Transfer's LF-pretraining partially bakes in a half-cell registration shift that fine-tuning must un-teach** (pre-fix benchmark) — VERIFIED mechanism identification on the OLD defective pipeline (PRE_ROUND1 review §4); the current family code routes through the repaired `lf_registration.py`, so this is a historical caveat, not a live property.
14. **fisher_kpp 6.4× loss is metric-sensitive**: rel-L2 is level-dominated there (copy-LF 0.00062 raw vs 0.02161 mean-removed, 35×) — VERIFIED for the metric property; implication for the model comparison NOT computed (benchmark30 report.md caveat 3; memory rel-l2-is-level-dominated).

## D. Existing related-work knowledge (from `mf_field/factory_mffp/paper/RELATED_WORK_RESEARCH.md`)

- **Novelty verdict** (§1): FiLM+FNO already exists; the unpublished combination is FiLM conditioning as the vehicle for LF→HF multi-fidelity TRANSFER in an FNO, for static parameter-vector→field prediction, plus the controlled concat-vs-FiLM ablation.
- **Closest prior art**: Zhu, Raccuglia & Brenner 2025, "Generalizing PDE Emulation with Equation-Aware Neural Operators", arXiv:2511.09729 ("FiLMed FNO", 7-dim equation encoding) — but autoregressive time-stepping, zero-shot equation generalization, verified to contain NO multi-fidelity and NO transfer learning.
- **Second-closest**: Shokar, Kerswell & Haynes 2025, arXiv:2509.09599 — FiLM-via-adaptive-LayerNorm with zero-init γ/δ and pretrain→finetune, closely paralleling the film design, but on a local-attention Transformer (not FNO), for time-stepping, with cross-domain-size (not LF/HF) pretraining; miscited in the repo as `beggs2025pdecond` (fix pending).
- **Original FiLM**: Perez et al. 2018, arXiv:1709.07871.
- **Conditioning taxonomy** (§2): constant-channel concat (standard; Lyu 2023) | FiLM/affine modulation (Zhu, Shokar, ours) | hypernetwork→weights (Zhu; HyPINO) | spectral gating (Zhu "LSC-FNO") | cross-attention (Zhu; SpectraKAN arXiv:2602.05187).
- **MF-operator families and their fidelity mechanism** (§3): transfer (Lyu arXiv:2304.06972; carbon-storage arXiv:2308.09113) | residual/additive (MFRNP, MF-DeepONet, Demo/Tezzele/Rozza) | coregionalization (IFC) | discretization-invariance | multi-resolution active learning (MRA-FNO); "fidelity is rarely a FiLM input" — the gap film fills.
- **Pre-drafted reviewer rebuttals** (§4): (1) "FiLMed FNO exists" → boundary drawn on task+MF axes; (2) "FNO wins smooth trivially" → regime split; (3) "capacity?" → identical backbone + 2M control; (4) "prior conditioning benchmark?" → none isolates the mechanism controlled.
- Additional prior-art intel in `docs/reports/MF_FNO_CNN_Hybrid_Report.md` §6: Park et al. arXiv:2507.06133 (S-DeepONet + FiLM-modulated diffusion refiner — the sole verified FiLM user among refiner papers), SINO arXiv:2606.18305, CorrDiff, U-FNO/U-AFNO/LOGLO-FNO/HUFNO/WHNO; verdict that "FiLM applied to an FNO" may be the one genuinely unclaimed combination, observed as an absence, never searched directly.
- `docs/reports/MF_Leaderboard_Beaters_2026_Report.md` supplies the 2026 threat landscape (IRNO ICML'26 spotlight, F-Adapter NeurIPS'25, MFFM, NOWS/NPO predict-then-solve, etc.) — useful for the discussion section, saved mainly for the other paper.

## E. Caveats/defects a paper must disclose

From `mffp_autoresearch/PRE_ROUND1_BENCHMARK_REVIEW_2026-08-05.md` (applies to the 42-dataset pre-round-1 ELO artifact, which predates the 2026-08-01 registration repair):

1. On 7 of 42 datasets (era5, pm_test, 5 sharp 1-D sets) the scored TARGET itself went through the defective half-cell-shifted bilinear map — models were scored against "a blurred, half-shifted rendering of the solution" (§3); sizing: target distortion rel-L2 ~0.0142 vs model errors 0.21–0.48 on the sharp-1D sets (integrity defect, not the cause of failures), but era5/pm_test additionally alias (3.2% of pixels retained).
2. The LF training signal was defective for ALL 12 families in that benchmark (shifted pretraining target; §4) — the transfer families literally pretrained to emit a half-cell-shifted field.
3. The champion is not robust to dataset scope: film #1 on all-40, `convnext_unet_film` #1 on non-sharp-20 and core-15 (§2).
4. **era5 and pm_test are byte-identical duplicates** (md5-verified; 8/9 models bit-identical rel-L2), so era5 was double-weighted in every ELO (§6); `datasets_summary.csv` now records `alias_of=era5` on pm_test — the paper-era "15 datasets" includes both, so the 15-dataset leaderboard double-counts era5 too.
5. On the 2-D sharp panel, every model loses to a zero-parameter corrected copy-LF resample by 4×–147× — the ELO there ranks degrees of failure (§5).
6. `sharp__phase_field_crystal_2d` had essentially no fidelity gap (fine-grid energy outside the coarse band 4.9e-16) — its matches are noise (§7); the pfc defect was later corrected via regeneration (git status shows a 2026-08-12 pfc refresh; memory: registration-fix note / ADR r3-0002).
7. `ext__helmholtz_2d` and `sharp__helmholtz_2d` reproduce from the condition vector in closed form to 2.2e-13 via two FFTs — cannot support a multi-fidelity claim (§7).
8. Per-family target-scaling defects (global max|y| vs per-sample) hit helmholtz/pfc rankings as per-model noise (§7).
9. Only 4 of 19 sharp datasets were PROVEN node-sampled at review time; `transolver_residual`'s registration convention was untraced and its metric is val-flagged (§10).

From the benchmark_30 campaign (`benchmark30/docs/report.md` "Data-methodology caveats"; PROFESSOR_UPDATE §2):

10. `ext__pressure_poisson_poiseuille`'s LF is block-mean-downsampled HF + noise — violates the repo's "LF is always a real coarse solve" rule (inherited from the source paper's own construction).
11. `heat_generated`'s fidelity levels sample inconsistent time windows (LF→HF conflates resolution transfer with time-window shift).
12. rel-L2 is level-dominated on near-uniform fields: sharp__fisher_kpp_2d copy-LF 0.00062 raw vs 0.02161 mean-removed (35×) — the 6.4× film loss there is protocol-true but metric-sensitive; mean-removed model outputs were NOT computed.
13. era5 is excluded from the head-to-head common set (r3s2 grid cap) and has ragged per-rung train counts.
14. Benchmark self-corrections on record (PROFESSOR_UPDATE §3): the pfc no-fidelity-gap discovery, and a checkpoint-resume defect that silently skipped retraining several baselines (32 runs quarantined and retrained; a checkpoint↔data binding gate now guards builds).
15. Paper-draft-internal TODOs that are themselves disclosures (paper_draft.md Appendix A): lid-driven cavity v1 HF was non-converged (v2 exists, rows not updated); the `beggs2025pdecond` citation is wrong; several related-work arXiv IDs unverified.
16. Three inconsistent ELO numbers exist for film (1936 paper-era 16-way; 1820 30-family zoo; 1692 42-dataset pre-round-1) — any paper must pin exactly one benchmark artifact and scope per claim.
17. Round-3/benchmark_30 head-to-head ran at 200 epochs vs the paper-era 2500 epochs; the epoch-ladder pilot testing whether film's bar moves with budget is incomplete (§B5).

## G. Gaps: what a "why does film win" interpretability paper needs that does NOT yet exist

Diagnostics never run (all confirmed absent from the documents above):

1. **No probe of the learned FiLM parameters themselves.** Nothing anywhere inspects γ(X)/β(X): their magnitude per block, per-channel structure, dependence on X components, drift between LF-pretrain and HF-finetune, or how far γ deviates from the identity init. This is the core interpretability object and it is completely unmeasured.
2. **No conditioning-site ablations.** No experiment varies WHERE FiLM is applied (first block only, last block only, subset of blocks), the FiLM MLP width, or FiLM-without-GroupNorm — the paper's "re-injected at every block" story has no per-block attribution.
3. **No controlled comparison against the rest of the conditioning zoo** (hypernetwork, spectral gating, cross-attention) on the same backbone — flagged as TODO in paper_draft.md Appendix A and limitation (3).
4. **The k=0 spectral-inertness claim is untested.** No measurement of how much the concat model's spectral layers actually act on the constant condition channels (e.g. energy of condition information by mode, or probing accuracy of X from activations at each depth in both models).
5. **No transfer/warm-start analysis.** Nothing measures what the LF pretraining actually transfers: weight-space distance pre/post finetune, CKA/representation similarity, LF-pretrain vs random-init ablation per dataset (does film win even without pretraining?), or whether FiLM specifically protects pretrained features during finetuning.
