# Research record and stopping decision

Research cutoff: September 14, 2026. This is a targeted narrative review for a working
manuscript, not a preregistered systematic review or an exhaustive priority search.
This record summarizes the completed search phases; it does not invent exact query
strings or source-access timestamps that were not retained.

## Search phases and decisions

1. **Scope and format.** Read the local findings, machine-readable mechanism/fresh-heat
   summaries, repair protocol, input audit and generator issues. Checked official ICLR
   2027 author guidance, downloaded the official style archive and checked AI-use policy.
2. **Two independent literature lanes.** Existing research agents reviewed
   AutoML/stacking/ensemble selection and multifidelity/operators/climate applications.
   The coordinator integrated their findings and independently checked the most
   consequential collisions against primary sources.
3. **Classical combination methods.** Followed citations to Wolpert, Breiman, Super
   Learner, Caruana and Goel. Checked auto-sklearn and AutoGluon venue/metadata.
   Decision: the mixture objective and automation are established components; require
   an identical-library greedy ensemble selection control.
4. **Adaptive multifidelity collisions.** Read EL-MFS publisher material, AQBMF's
   author-hosted full paper and MAESTRO's indexed SSRN record. Resolved the apparent
   future-publication problem by verifying the February 2026 preprint. Decision:
   abandon any broad “first automatic multifidelity ensemble” claim.
5. **Field-specific automation.** Read LANE-SI full HTML and HyperNOs primary material.
   Learned spatial-field combinations and automated operator HPO already exist.
   Do not use the unverified AutoNO name. Position the paper by its joint practical
   setting and evidence, without inferring priority from a search omission.
6. **Multifidelity architectures and climate.** Checked MFRNP full HTML/proceedings,
   MF-DeepONet, MF-FNO/WNO, Faza and FIRE. Audited how local adapters differ from cited
   originals. Checked ERA5/ClimateBench distinctions and climate internal-variability
   work. Decision: require simple linear/pattern-scaling climate baselines; call the
   ERA5 task climate emulation rather than operational weather forecasting.
7. **Mathematics and evidence.** Read Breiman's Gram formulation and the original
   ensemble ambiguity source; checked Hoeffding metadata and the elementary bound.
   Independently regenerated 27 aggregates, the 15-dataset audit sensitivity, all
   plotted data and per-dataset tables. Kept historical, fresh and pending evidence
   distinct; did not substitute component scores for the complete selector.
8. **Citation closure.** Reconciled 33 BibTeX entries with primary metadata, including
   proceedings versus workshops and online/preprint versus issue dates. Final narrow
   checks covered the AutoML records and Goel's institutional record.

## Stopping decision

Stop broad searching after direct precedents resolve the consequential novelty
questions and the targeted follow-ups resolve publication-date and field-output
scope ambiguities. More searching could uncover further related work but would
not justify restoring the rejected “first” claims. Remaining blockers are mainly
experimental: the full frozen selector, repaired climate, identical-library greedy
selection, total HF/compute-matched controls and independent confirmation.

Access limitations are explicit: MAESTRO full text was inaccessible; some older
publisher papers were available only as abstracts/metadata or author copies.
Claims from limited-access sources are restricted to that evidence. No inaccessible
source is treated as a completed detailed quantitative comparison.
