# Revised reporting scope on 16 September 2026

At the author's request, remove the former Helmholtz I dataset
(`ext__helmholtz_2d`) from the current manuscript, all reported comparisons,
figures, tables, and derived reporting data. The decision follows inspection of
its high historical errors. It is not a newly identified numerical defect or a
prospectively specified exclusion. The discussion identifies the retrospective
scope decision.

The paper now contains 18 datasets: 17 PDE datasets in seven descriptive classes,
and ERA5 on its separate evaluation split. The PDE analysis uses 51 calibration
partitions and 153 automatic rule fits across three budgets. It contains 1,614
evaluation appearances, 1,317 distinct dataset and row pairs, and 2,680 historical
query rows. The baseline comparison has 219 verified model and dataset cells.

The remaining Helmholtz and Cahn Hilliard entries have no numeral. Cavity was
already unnumbered. Poisson I/II and Heat I/II each still have two entries.
Figure 2 and the enlarged gallery are reordered consistently, including their
captions and imported data citations.

At five calibration examples, the fitted mixture improves on the calibration
selected model on 12 PDE datasets and worsens on five. The geometric error
reductions are 12.4 percent across PDE classes and 8.2 percent across datasets.
Seven library members, rather than eight, attain the lowest mean error on at
least one retained PDE dataset. Every aggregate, coverage subset, fitting loss
control, and associated textual claim is updated for the revised denominator.

Underlying archived predictions, source data, historical fit records, and the
original manuscript are preserved. Their dataset identifiers and dated revision
records are provenance, not the current reporting roster. The retained model,
baseline, and mixture errors are unchanged. ERA5 results are unchanged.

Validation: data regeneration, LaTeX compilation, and all paper verification
checks pass. The main text remains nine pages, with 42 pages including references
and appendices. Main pages 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and appendix page 38 were visually
inspected, together with the changed gallery plates and aggregate plots.
