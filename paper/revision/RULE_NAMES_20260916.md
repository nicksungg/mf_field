Its equation uses S, I, and F, explicitly associated with the full names, in place of unexplained alternative textual labels.

A shared display naming helper keeps generated tables consistent while preserving the original numerical method identifiers.

This reflects automatic selection choosing the fitted mixture on those inputs. The example Heat I figure continues to show Automatic selection equal to the Inverse error mixture.
