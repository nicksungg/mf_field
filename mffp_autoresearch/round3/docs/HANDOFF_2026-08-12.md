## Open item 2 (needs a ruling) — panel multiplicity and the mis-calibrated bar

These are one problem, not two, and they must be taken in this order.

### The panel multiplicity

Five different panels coexist in the round-3 record and are not distinguished at the point of use:

| Name | Composition |
|---|---|
| **registered-4** | `allen_cahn`, `fisher_kpp`, `cahn_hilliard`, `ifc_heat` |
| **scored-5** (ADR r3-0007) | as published in ADR 0008 |
| **6-cell** | includes `ifc_poisson`, which is report-only |
| **sharp-4** | the four sharp datasets |
| **5-cell anchor-comparand** | used against the stream anchor |

`registered-4` is the one carrying the batch-3 headline in the report.
Report §2 already states that two of its rows "cannot be ranked against each other", so the multiplicity is acknowledged but not resolved.

### The integrity defect that follows from it

`state/anchors_repaired/noise_floor.json` calibrates `seed_mce = 0.5083` on the panel
`['ifc_heat', 'ifc_poisson', 'sharp__allen_cahn_2d', 'sharp__cahn_hilliard', 'sharp__fisher_kpp_2d']`.
That set **includes report-only `ifc_poisson` and omits scored `pfc`** — it matches no current panel composition.

The bar is therefore read against subsets it was not calibrated on, which contradicts the round's own rule at report §8: *"calibrate every subset bar on the subset it is read against."*

The audit tool cannot catch this.
`tools/subset_geomean_unit_audit.py` computes its verdict as
`"OK" if sorted(cells) == sorted(panel)`, where `panel` is the **declared `--panel` CLI argument**, not the set the bar was actually calibrated on.
It compares the claim to itself.

Confirmed by three independent checks: the audit verdict logic, report line 51's 6-cell geomean including report-only `ifc_poisson`, and the calibration panel matching no live composition.

### What is needed

**ADR 0008 recommends Option C** — keep the cell membership, fix the aggregation and its bar, and add a concentration rule.
The supporting arithmetic: `ifc_heat` alone carries $59.0\%$ of the registered-4 headline, and removing `ifc_poisson` *raises* the top-cell share from $55.7\%$ to $60.3\%$, so a composition change alone does not fix concentration.

Under Option C the direction of the headline is unaffected; what changes is which forms are claim-bearing.
The six-cell $14.1909$ and the panel6 $-4.2448$ / $8.35\times$ statements retire, and the licensed forms become scored-5 and registered-4 re-read against a correctly calibrated bar.

Sequence once ruled:

1. Ratify ADR 0008.
2. Recalibrate the bar on whichever subsets the ruling makes claim-bearing.
3. Fix `subset_geomean_unit_audit.py` so the verdict compares against the calibration panel rather than the declared one — the current check cannot fail.
4. Correct the affected report statements.

Step 3 was done 2026-08-12, ahead of the ruling, since the audit was tautological under all of the options (commit `2b72977`).
`subset_geomean_unit_audit.py` now takes the calibration set from `--bar-calibration-json` (the anchor's `_panel_geomean.panel`) or `--bar-panel`, emits `UNVERIFIED_BAR_CALIBRATION` when given neither, and the declared panel itself can fail; `tools/tests/test_subset_geomean_unit_audit.py` replays the R$_1$-vs-R$_0$ inversion and is mutation-verified against the reverted verdict line.
Steps 1, 2 and 4 remain contingent on the ruling.
