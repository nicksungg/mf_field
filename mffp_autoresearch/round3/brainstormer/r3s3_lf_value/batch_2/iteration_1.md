## Proposal reasoning

**Why the cap ladder is the right slot rather than a new mechanism.**
The generic §12.3 menu (distillation / LF-pretrain→HF-finetune / auxiliary MF losses) is exhausted for this stream at this point: the auxiliary channel is a *certified null* (r2s4-B2), the teacher channel is `preempted (cite)` twice, and the pretrain→finetune channel is a declared baseline whose re-run is a rebadge. What batch 1 actually opened, and what the card itself pre-directed, is the *pricing* of the one live channel. Proposing anything from the exhausted menu would be a self-check failure on item 10; proposing the ladder discharges the card's own recorded debt.

**Alternative A — ladder only (rejected as insufficient).**
A pure LF-cap sweep reproduces exactly what the verdict forbids claiming: "The **LF-count sweep genre is published — do not claim it** ... The finding must be the measured knee and exchange rate, never the sweep." A ladder with no HF-budget ladder cannot produce an exchange rate, and with no mediator regression cannot produce a law. Rejected: it would deliver a table inside a published genre.

**Alternative B — a separate diagnostic card for the 27-row mask (rejected as wasteful and weaker).**
C3 is the websearcher's "most defensible novelty", but it is a *per-row stratification of predictions the ladder legs already dump*. Splitting it into its own card would (i) cost a second training campaign for zero new training, and (ii) leave the ladder without the population-dependence control that decides whether the headline knee is an average of two mechanisms. Folding it in as a stratified readout on the same dumps is strictly better and costs zero extra legs. Rejected as a standalone card; **adopted as deliverable D-D of this card**.

**Alternative C — re-run the full 6-dataset panel with the fixed scaler (rejected on scope).**
Attractive because the scaler bug touches every dataset, but part 7 explicitly narrows to the two datasets "where the coverage channel is real AND unconfounded", ifc_poisson is evidence-backed dropped, and a panel re-run would spend the batch re-measuring a decomposition instead of pricing it. The scaler repair is still *carried* — it is applied to ch and ifc_heat and its arm-invariance is asserted — so the fix is validated where it will be claimed. Rejected on scope; the panel-wide re-scaler is left as a batch-3/audit hand-off.

**Alternative D — 1 HF draw instead of 3 (rejected on reproducibility).**
Cheaper (21 fewer ch legs/seed), but B1 turn 3 measured the ch A3s draw means at 0.3689 / 0.1202 / 0.2761 with F(2,6) = 5.32 and a 0.640 between-draw variance share — a knee located on a single draw would not be reproducible. Rejected: the ladder runs 3 HF draws × 3 training seeds = 9 cells per cap on ch, which is what makes the knee a measurement rather than an anecdote.

**Alternative E — a distillation/teacher arm as the "LF-as-training-signal" mechanism (rejected on prior art).**
Batch-1 D3 and round-2 batch 1 both returned `preempted (cite)`; the websearcher repeats it in instruction 6 ("any teacher/distillation arm is `preempted`, baseline only"). Rejected outright.

**Why the scaler fix is load-bearing, not housekeeping.**
`smoke_eval.py:491-495` computes `per_rung_max` over "exactly the rows this arm trains on for that rung". A cap ladder varies precisely those rows, so the scaler would drift *monotonically with the cap* and the entire cap curve would be partly a normalisation curve. B1 measured the size of this on ch: s_rung/s_hf = 1.169–1.174 for the full-pool arms vs 1.000 for the 5-row arms. Hence a new scaler mode **`per_rung_max_fullpool`** — max|y| over the full rung pool of the stripped train view, independent of arm and cap — with a hard assertion that the scaler is bit-identical across every arm and cap of a (dataset, rung) pair. This also re-certifies B1's headline decomposition under a matched procedure (round-2 §6 arm-comparison rule).

**Why the exchange rate needs its own ladder.**
A single A4 point (N_hf 20) brackets the rate between "8 %" and "59 %" of the LF pool — a factor of 7. An HF ladder N_hf ∈ {5,10,20,40} against the LF ladder gives the rate as a bracketed interval at every recovery level, priced against `tau_rel`. ch has 400 shipped HF train rows so this uses only already-shipped data; ifc_heat is native N_hf = 5 with no spare HF rows, so **the exchange rate is declared a ch-only deliverable up front** rather than being quietly reported as panel-wide.

**Why the alignment mediator is the novelty lever.**
The verdict's open clause (d) — "the sweep is regressed on a mediating learned quantity ... not just error" — plus instruction 4 ("Regress recovery on alignment, not just on row count ... nothing retrieved reports a mediating learned quantity for MF sample-count curves"). The sharp, falsifiable version is a **collapse test**: if the HF-budget legs land on the *same* R(alignment) curve as the LF-cap legs, then LF rows and HF rows are interchangeable *through one measured intermediate*, and the exchange rate has a mechanism. That is a prediction that can fail, and its failure is itself reportable.

**Terminology decision (C3 hazard).** The subpopulation is named **forward-sensitive subpopulation** throughout, defined inline on first use ("condition-space-indistinguishable — max per-dim standardised gap 0.4476 σ, nearest-train-neighbour ratio 1.0176 — but field-distant, nearest-train-field rel-L2 ratio 3.29"). The words "non-identifiable" / "unidentifiable" are banned from the card (round-2 §6 phrasing rule + the PLOS inverse-usage hazard). Because the r3s1 mask was derived from *that stream's* per-row rel-L2 bimodality (model-dependent), the card imports it **and** independently re-derives a model-free mask (top-27 ch test rows by nearest-train-HF-field rel-L2), reports the overlap, and stratifies under both if they disagree.

**Datasets.** `sharp__cahn_hilliard` (primary, reference-free per ADR r3-0003 D2) + `ifc_heat` (corroboration + affine-floor reporting per program §2). `ifc_poisson` dropped on B1's evidence. `sharp__phase_field_crystal_2d` is report-only under ADR r3-0004 and is **not enumerated in any clause**.

**Budget.** Per training seed: ch 39 legs (7 caps × 3 draws = 21; A0/A1/A2 × 3 draws = 9; HF ladder {10,20,40} × 3 draws = 9) + ifc_heat 10 legs (7 caps + A0/A1/A2) + 1 guard = **50 legs/seed**, 3 jobs (one per training seed), **150 legs total**. At B1's measured 2.4 min/leg (60 legs in 140–150 min, `state/timing_ledger.json`) this is ~2.0 h/seed; `--time 05:00:00` gives 2.5× preemption headroom, with `--mail-type=END,FAIL`.

### Deliverables (each with its own resolvability statement)

- **D-A knee.** R(c) = (nRMSE(A0) − nRMSE(arm@c)) / (nRMSE(A0) − nRMSE(A1_lf_all)), reference-free on ch, aggregated over 9 cells/cap. Knee c\* = smallest ladder rung with mean R ≥ 0.90 whose next-lower rung is below it by more than `tau_rel`. ch resolution: 0.015101 nRMSE = **2.0–2.9 %** of the effect. ifc_heat resolution: 0.012137 nRMSE = **21.8 %** of the effect (coarse — declared corroboration only).
- **D-B floor-crossing cap.** Smallest cap at which the trained model beats the dataset's best training-free floor (ch `nn_condition` 0.96901; ifc_heat `affine_on_hf_train` 0.070918 / `nn_condition` 0.10316). A distinct quantity from D-A and, on ch, arguably the deployable one.
- **D-C exchange rate (ch only).** Bracketed interval: the LF cap range whose mean R matches R at N_hf ∈ {10,20,40}, each bracket priced against `tau_rel`. Declared unmeasurable on ifc_heat (native N_hf = 5).
- **D-D single-mediator collapse.** Pooled Pearson/Spearman of R on alignment across all LF-cap and HF-budget cells, plus the residual of the HF-budget points about the LF-ladder fit.
- **D-E forward-sensitive stratification (ch).** R_hard(c) vs R_easy(c) on the 27/73 split, both masks, per-row deltas, reference-free.
- **D-F re-certified decomposition.** E_cov/E_total and E_opt on ch + ifc_heat under `per_rung_max_fullpool`, replacing B1's scaler-confounded ch figure.
