## Proposal reasoning — what changed and why

**FLAG A — close.** Immutable §5.10 makes the reviewer ask "is this a rebadge of a round-1 family?" on every card, and round-3 inherits it. The exposure is real: `direct_*` arms resemble `r2s1_direct`'s FiLM-FNO condition->HF head. Four changes remove it, all card-side rather than reviewer-side:

1. The family's new pathway is declared explicitly as **a single trunk behind a route switch plus a budget accountant that holds total optimizer epochs equal across routes, with a regularised LSI transfer stage** — an object that exists in neither r2s1 nor r2s2/r3s2-B1.
2. The `direct_*` arms are declared **matched controls inside the contrast**, not candidate architectures, and are newly implemented in this family; **no code is vendored from `r2s1_direct`** (only from `r3s2_stack_ic` @ `d5069a74`, which is this stream's own B1).
3. The differentiator is stated numerically: the direct arms carry the **zero-parameter analytic IC-synthesis channel** (r2s1's heads are FiLM-only on the raw condition vector) and are **budget-locked** to the stack at `E_emu + 2*E_dc = 300` optimizer epochs.
4. `A4 direct_film` is pre-registered as the **r2s1 calibration arm** — expected near r2s1_direct-B2's certified **23.7753**. If it lands far from that, the family (not the route) is the story, and the card says so before the run.
Declared reuse remains **role (a)** (round-2 §5.10a): the DC-lineage corrector is a frozen test-time sub-component; the reuse IS the experiment, exactly as B1 declared.

**FLAG B — close.** The card's hypothesis H is the **route** hypothesis. The LSI repair is demoted in wording throughout to an **instrument acceptance gate** (clause G2), with the verdict quoted on the card so no reader can mistake it for a contribution: *"usable ONLY as an instrument repair, never as a contribution"* and *"Presenting ridge/band-limiting as the *idea* is a rebadge"*. G2's legs are pre-registered predictions of a repair's effect, plus a replication check on the unrepaired replay arm.

**FLAG C — close.** The two routes do not see the same training rows on the ifc cells: the stack's stage 1 fits **18 of 20 LF rows** while any direct head sees **5 HF rows** (both read from B1's `diag_ifc_*_e200_s0.json`). Three consequences are written into the design:
- `R3S2B2_ROUTE_DATA_DISCLOSURE=1` records `n_train_route` per arm per dataset in the diag and the card's part-5 table.
- Every ifc route delta is reported **with** that disclosure; the raw ifc delta is explicitly NOT the ifc headline.
- The ifc headline is the zero-GPU **`A6` LOO selector** over {`affine_on_hf_train`, `A1`, `A3`} on the 5 HF train rows (part 7 item 4), which is the only ifc design that is not anecdote-grade under round-2 §12.1.
This also sharpens the science rather than weakening it: the asymmetry is precisely the regime in which arXiv:2512.02868 predicts the MF stack should win ("particularly in data-scarce scenarios"), against B1's measurement that it loses a 5-row closed form by 1.63x-35.6x. The card pre-registers both readings.

**Alternatives re-weighed this pass** (and still rejected): E1-as-headline (rebadge per verdict); a ch capacity card (M7 + websearcher item 5); an ifc-only card (§12.1 anecdote-grade, `mdd_scored = 0`, E3 preempts the instrument); a decoder swap (confounds the route with the architecture); re-running `end_to_end` as primary (B1's apparent win is a shadow of the seed-2 defect, M1).
