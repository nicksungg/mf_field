## 6. What is UNKNOWN

1. **Whether the completeness repair lifts the round-2 ceiling at all.** Round-2's terminal diagnosis was explicitly conditional: the ceiling holds *if* the missing variable is the realised IC. Round 3 exports that variable (16 `ic_c*` dims on pfc/ac/ch, 48 on fk; `condition_completeness.reconstruction.rel_L2_max = 0.0` in every sharp `meta.json`). The certified anchors already answer part of this: r2s2-B1 **retrained on the repaired arrays** still sits at 27.5068 — so completeness alone, consumed through a FiLM-on-condition path, bought little. What is unknown is whether that is an *information* verdict or a *conditioning-path* verdict.
2. **Whether a FiLM MLP can use 16-48 IC coefficients at all.** Round 2 measured the shared FiLM conditioning path collapsing to eff-rank **1.00/1.00/1.00/1.00** of 128 channels on helmholtz while the underlying map was continuous (nearest-neighbour band>=1 cosine +0.931). If that collapse recurs at 18-50 dims, "the condition is complete" and "the model can read the condition" are different statements, and no round has separated them.
3. **Whether support, not information, is now binding.** Median standardized pair distance is 5.95 (pfc), 6.13 (ac), 9.98 (fk) at 400 train rows; round-2's ch emulator memorised (in-sample 0.0317 -> held-out 0.4649) at exactly this support. An exact analytic IC channel converts 16-48 interpolation dimensions into a *field* the network sees directly — untested, and the only lever that bypasses the support problem without new data (§5.11 forbids new data).
4. **What the corrector is worth once its input is realisation-faithful.** Round 2 could not answer this: its emulator provably emitted E[LF | c] (the realisation was absent from c), and its frozen-vs-finetuned contrast was budget-confounded (F8). A budget-matched re-run under completeness is the only way to price the stack's second stage honestly.
5. **The ifc columns.** Round 2's ifc_poisson attribution was `valid=false` (unpaired ladder, `dc_fit_source='pseudo_lf(unpaired_real_lf)'`); the ladders are now repaired (`pairing_report.json`, row-match 1.0) and ifc_heat is newly scored — the stack has never run on a paired ifc ladder, and it currently loses to the `affine_on_hf_train` floor on both (21.14 vs 1.5938; 1.5737 vs 0.9584).
6. **The noise floor itself.** `state/anchors_repaired/noise_floor.json` is PROVISIONAL (`_source: round1-batch0-rescaled`, `_provisional: true`): pfc 8.8595, ac 16.4223, fk 158.3322, ch 1.1604, ifc_poisson 0.2399, **no ifc_heat entry and no panel-geomean entry**. r3s4-B1 re-certifies it; round-2's certified ch mce (0.0912) was 12x *smaller* than its provisional one, so any threshold written now must be stated against whichever floor is in force at analysis time, with the provisional values quoted as the interim.
7. **Denominator caveats that bound any claim here**: pfc's crystalline-box swap (reference 0.018257, stiff-map ~1e4 linear amplification), fisher_kpp's weak gap (median 2.0e-3, at the gap curve's peak), ac's trimmed test split (n = 78), ch's outlier domination (top-5 rows = 76% of the denominator, min-detectable ~166%).
