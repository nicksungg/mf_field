### Turn 2 — the MF corpus, and model selection on the deployment distribution
Terms: (1) MF surrogate with the LF solver replaced by a learned emulator, error-budget attribution; (2) selecting a regularisation hyperparameter on predicted inputs / covariate-shift model selection; (3) MF-GP stage-wise / substituted-LF ablation.
Key findings: **second engine-level concession** that the MF composition is not asked; nearest MF neighbour is *"Replacing the true model with an approximation inevitably introduces errors in posterior sampling, whose magnitude depends on the approximation error"* — but with the LF model still evaluated and the accounting probabilistic [cite: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11683v1]. ### Turn 3 — the textbook name, and the canonical crossed design
Terms: (1) two-stage TTS vocoder on predicted vs ground-truth mels; (2) `"ceiling analysis"` pipelines.
Key findings: Tacotron 2 §3.3.1 *"Predicted Features versus Ground Truth"* runs the **full 2×2 crossed matrix** (train ∈ {predicted, GT} × synthesise from ∈ {predicted, GT}; MOS 4.526/4.449/4.362/4.522) and explains the asymmetry by the upstream's **oversmoothing**; its default recipe already trains the downstream stage *"on the ground truth-aligned predictions of the feature prediction network"* [cite: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.05884]. The frozen / fine-tuned / joint arm ladder is likewise standard: *"since the acoustic model and the vocoder are not jointly optimized, **a gap can exist between the two models**"* [cite: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.02867]. "Ceiling analysis" (replace a component with perfect predictions, read the gain) confirmed as a named method — snippet-only, IEEE page blocked. Recorded **ENOUGH** on field context here. → `iteration_3.md`

### Turn 4 — refutation pass 1: is "fit on real, deploy on generated" named in a field science?
Terms: (1) perfect prognosis vs model output statistics; (2) SR trained on real coarse, applied to emulator-generated coarse.
Key findings: yes, and for decades — *"**Once the relationship is established in these 'perfect' conditions, we feed the model/algorithm with the equivalent GCM predictor variables**"* [cite: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.00974]; MOS is the standing complement (fit on the model's own output). SciML states the ceiling but does not measure it: *"it is generally necessary that the low-resolution input data is accurate on its own coarse grid … **The super-resolved field would not be physically accurate if the low-resolution flow field (input) is deviated from the true solution**"* [cite: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.10937]. → `iteration_4.md`

### Turn 5 (CAP) — refutation pass 2 + verdict
