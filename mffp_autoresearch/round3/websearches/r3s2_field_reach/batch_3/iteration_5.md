**Fetched — arXiv:2602.13416** (HTML body, 86,829 chars). The setup is our stack with climate nouns: an upstream learned emulator (LUCIE, an SFNO climate emulator) produces the coarse state; a downstream super-resolver (U-Net / SFNO-SR / conditional EDM / diffusion posterior sampling) maps coarse → fine. Verbatim, the decisive sentences:

- The downstream stage is fit on the REAL coarse field and deployed on the GENERATED one, deliberately: *"we **deliberately retain a downscaler trained purely on reanalysis** and instead evaluate how faithfully it preserves the dynamics of an independent emulator … **without any debiasing or domain-adaptation step**"*; *"The SR models are trained on ERA5 reanalysis data (2000–2009) … and subsequently evaluated on their ability to super-resolve outputs from the LUCIE emulator."*
- And the oracle arm exists, with the card's exact stated purpose: *"**To disentangle errors introduced by the downscaling step from those inherited from the LUCIE emulator, we additionally evaluate the diffusion models in a perfect-prognosis (PP) setting, in which they downscale coarsened ERA5 inputs rather than LUCIE outputs**… In this configuration the input coarse state is, **by construction, free of emulator bias** and internal-variability mismatch with the ERA5 target."* Result direction: *"For the Conditional EDM the resulting climatological bias maps are substantially smaller and spatially smoother than those of the corresponding LUCIE[-fed rows]."*
- They also name the failure mode and cite prior work on it: *"a crucial and often overlooked challenge arises when models trained on 'perfect' coarsened reanalysis data are applied to inputs from an independent climate model or emulator. This **'imperfect input' setting can degrade spatial consistency and underestimate extremes** when inference-time coarse forcing differs statistically from the coarsened training data (ReddyEtAl2025_LimitationSR)"*, with *"prior work that incorporates explicit debiasing, domain adaptation, or bias correction to handle the reanalysis-to-model gap (wan2023debias; wan2024regional; aich2026conditional)"*.
- Their diagnostic panel is also ours: latitude-weighted RMSE, **power spectra / high-wavenumber variance**, PDFs, and EOF modes — i.e. band-resolved reporting, matching the standard batch 2 already imported.

**Fetched — Womack et al., ESD 17, 107, 2026** (<https://esd.copernicus.org/articles/17/107/2026/>, 161,439 chars): a *within-emulator* error taxonomy — *"a theoretical framework that connects disparate emulation techniques and use it to understand potential sources of emulator error focusing on **memory effects, hidden variables, system noise, and nonlinearities**"*. Relevant as the nearest published "sources of error in emulation" framework, but it decomposes an emulator's own error by physical cause; it does **not** split a stacked emulator+downstream error.

*Preempted by (fetched this loop)*: <https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13416> — same ladder, same frozen-downstream design, same stated purpose ("to disentangle errors introduced by the downscaling step from those inherited from the LUCIE emulator"), on physical fields, with band-resolved diagnostics; <https://arxiv.org/html/2604.12440> — *"the oracle–predicted gap directly quantifies deployment readiness"* with a frozen upstream expert and an oracle → predicted → degraded-input ladder; <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07718> — *"oracle ablation experiments injecting ground truth inputs"* to see *"how end-to-end failure is distributed across stages"*; <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.05884> §3.3.1 — the crossed train-on-{GT, predicted} × evaluate-on-{GT, predicted} matrix; <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.10937> — the ceiling principle stated for coarse-input super-resolution. Snippet-only corroboration that the generic method has a textbook name ("ceiling analysis": replace a component with perfect predictions and read the gain) — <https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6521941/> (fetch blocked, title only), <https://timewithai.wordpress.com/2019/07/09/ceiling-analysis-in-machine-learning/>.
*What remains open, exactly*: in every fetched instance the oracle intermediate **exists in deployment** (coarsened ERA5, ground-truth masks, GT mel, a real coarse solve) — the PP arm is a *counterfactual input the domain can actually supply*. In this round's regime the LF field is available **at train only** and the intermediate must be hallucinated from a condition vector at test, so the ladder measures not a deployable alternative but a **bound on what the route could ever buy**. No fetched source (a) runs the ladder with an intermediate that is structurally unavailable at inference, (b) does it at N_hf = 5 with a closed-form/frozen corrector whose selection stage is enumerable over all C(5,3) folds, or (c) prices both rungs against a **certified training-free floor** and a certified baseline denominator in skill units. That triple is what the card may claim — the *measurement in this regime*, never the instrument.
