## Findings

### Term 1 — MF benchmark that certifies floor + reference uncertainty

Top results: "MAST: A Multi-fidelity Augmented Surrogate model via Spatial Trust-weighting" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20974); "Uncertainty-aware multi-fidelity surrogate modeling with noisy data" / "A comprehensive framework for multi-fidelity surrogate modeling with noisy data: a gray-box perspective" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06447, html https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06447v1); Bayesian-NN MF surrogate (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.02575); MF adaptive sampling (https://doi.org/10.3390/aerospace11060448); MF-SINDy (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15690).

What the MF-with-noisy-data line does (engine summary of 2401.06447 and the MAST benchmark pipeline): it **injects** Gaussian noise at each fidelity as a modeling assumption — *"the low-fidelity noise should inherit the high-fidelity baseline, so tightening the high-fidelity noise should automatically tighten the low-fidelity observations rather than leaving an independent low-fidelity floor fixed by construction"* — and propagates aleatoric + epistemic uncertainty into the surrogate's *predictive* distribution. That is uncertainty **in the model's prediction**, not uncertainty **in the benchmark's scoring reference**. No fetched or returned source certifies a per-dataset claim threshold from (i) measured seed spread of a trained baseline and (ii) the bootstrap sampling error of the reference cell that forms the metric's denominator. Nearest neighbors remain `SpecsVerification::SkillScore` (iteration 3, denominator uncertainty, but no seed/training-variance term and no MF setting) and the LLM-eval MDE atlas (iteration 1, seed/item variance, but no reference-normalized metric).

### Term 2 — degenerate MF test rows

**No usable results.** Three successive framings (multifidelity benchmark curation; ML benchmark degenerate-case curation; test-split curation generally) returned only unrelated matter: MF topology-optimization benchmarking (https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/19/4/769), NATO analytical MF benchmark problems (https://publications.sto.nato.int/publications/STO%20Meeting%20Proceedings/STO-MP-AVT-354/MP-AVT-354-02.pdf, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.07867), degenerate Pareto-front test problems in evolutionary multi-objective optimization (a different sense of "degenerate"), leakage-driven split curation (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15561 CTSCAN patient-disjoint benchmark), and generic benchmark-curation glossaries (https://truelabel.ai/glossary/benchmark-curation). The engine explicitly reported it *"was unable to find specific information about degenerate test cases where coarse solutions equal fine solutions being removed from multifidelity benchmark test split curation."* Combined with iteration 2's finding that the *aggregation* choice (per-sample relative error) is published with the anti-domination rationale but its near-zero-denominator failure mode is not audited, this is the one genuinely uncited spot in B1's scope.

### Term 3 — preflight in autonomous research pipelines

Top results: "XScientist: A Git-Like Research Protocol for Long-Running Autonomous Scientific Discovery" (https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12301); "Grounded autonomous research: a fault-tolerant LLM pipeline from corpus to manuscript in frontier computational physics" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02329); "BrainPilot: Automating Brain Discovery with Agentic Research" (https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15079v2); "Toward Pre-Deployment Assurance for Enterprise AI Agents" (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04037v1); agent-safety preflight listicle (https://medium.com/@ThinkingLoop/agent-safety-gates-12-preflight-checks-before-tools-run-ecf1c41ba252).

**Fetch of https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12301** (targeted extraction, ~150 words): XScientist frames long-running autonomous science around *"quality gating, daemon scheduling, and reproducibility artifacts as one"* protocol, and its stop conditions are stated verbatim: a run *"may stop because a **sample gate** fails, because a **truth contract** is violated, because **integrity forensics** finds hard flags, or because the operator pauses"*. *"Truth contracts translate a research plan into explicit constraints."* *"Before full generation, **sample gates** can require that a small planned task…"* (i.e. a cheap pilot must pass before the expensive run). It also has *"deterministic integrity forensics"* (§5.4), an artifact plane that *"records evidence, gates, repairs, and provenance"*, and daemon/budget controls. Structurally this is **the same architecture** as this round's `preflight.py` + `hold.py` + ADR trail + operator-clears-the-hold rule, published 2026-07. BrainPilot independently reports a *"skills-first preflight before adopting a nontrivial method"* and a pilot phase of *"program selection and tooling check, followed by reproductions of published references and an iterative gate-review computational sequence"*.
