We quarantined and retrained 32 runs, rebuilt the baselines, and added a permanent check to every baseline build.

After the baseline repair, no dataset in that experiment shows a resolvable change: the panel change is −0.81%, only 0.08× the certified minimum detectable effect. The experiment's mechanism decomposition remains valid and is its real result.
  • The mechanism tests replaced two preregistered explanations with better-supported ones (Fig. 2). The factorised output head succeeds when the first-stage residual saturates, not when the condition vector has a particular dimension. The benefit of initial-condition information arises entirely in stage 1; the corrective second stage again has no detectable effect.
  • The audit experiment established the round's minimum detectable effects and measured the reliability of the audit tools themselves. One checkpoint-staleness heuristic found 0 unique true positives but raised 62 false alarms across 859 runs; it is being replaced with content hashes stored in checkpoints.

1 · What round 3 asked, and how it ran

The learning regime is unchanged from round 2. Models receive low-fidelity (LF) fields during training but only the condition vector at test time; the test interface physically excludes LF fields.

The repaired benchmark now provides complete condition vectors and corrected fidelity hierarchies. Initial-condition coefficients are included in the condition vector (retiring round 2's stochastic-map caveat), the nested ifc fidelity hierarchies are repaired, and ifc_heat is now part of the evaluated set. The evaluated set is 5 datasets: allen_cahn, fisher_kpp, cahn_hilliard, ifc_poisson, ifc_heat (helmholtz and pfc are report-only).

Batch 1 covered four experiment lines: factorised heads, initial-condition reach, the value of LF training data, and benchmark auditing — each through the full sequence of literature search, preregistration, implementation, adversarial review, GPU execution, and two-stage analysis. The round also produced 5 architecture decision records and 7 new diagnostic tools; a stop-the-line repair consumed ~2 days.

The round now has certified minimum detectable effects. The batch-1 audit experiment established, per dataset, the smallest effect a claim may cite (seed-based minimum claimable effect plus its relative and absolute thresholds). The panel-level value is 0.5083; these thresholds govern every claim below.

Headline mechanism results (each from a causal probe, not a hunch)

  • The factorised head is distinguished by first-stage residual saturation, not condition dimension. allen_cahn and cahn_hilliard both have condition dimension 19, but their stage-2 margins differ 47×; fisher_kpp has the largest condition dimension (50) and the smallest margin. On fisher_kpp the loss to an affine reference is a hyperparameter limit: SELECT_MAX = 32 excludes 0.0713 of the condition-reachable energy. The cahn_hilliard improvement is broad, not outlier-driven: 78–82 of 100 rows improve, and it stays 2.9–3.2× above the minimum detectable effect after adversarially removing 10 rows.
  • Initial-condition information helps entirely through stage 1, and the remaining error is an approximation limit. The fractions of emulator error removed are 63.7% (allen_cahn) / 22.3% (cahn_hilliard) / 85.4% (fisher_kpp). A control given a fake, information-free initial condition performs worse than one given no initial condition at all, so the benefit comes from the information in the real initial conditions.
  • The corrective second stage is decisively ineffective for the second consecutive round: the best two-stage variant improves over its emulator-only control by 9–163× less than one minimum detectable effect; round 2's 0.0061 null replicates at 0.0056. Its one dramatic failure (ifc_poisson seed 2, error 2.04 vs 0.15) was a deterministic measurement defect: an unregularised spectral transfer function fitted from 3 samples, amplifying 66× in a frequency band where the LF input has no power.
  • LF training data helps by supplying new condition–response pairs, quantified. A control receiving extra LF data only at conditions already covered by HF learns the same function as the no-LF variant; new distinct condition points explain Ecov/Etotal ∈ [0.972, 1.024] in 30/30 evaluated cases. Recovery tracks a measurable intermediate (condition–response alignment, r = 0.985); batch 2 tests whether that mediator yields a stable exchange rate between LF and HF data points.
  • A seed-invariant hard subpopulation exists in cahn_hilliard: 27 of 100 test rows whose conditions are nearly indistinguishable from training (max gap 0.45σ) but whose fields are 3.29× farther away. LF training data specifically repairs this group (97% of that comparison's gain).

3 · Benchmark-integrity findings

  • The evaluated pfc task contained essentially no fidelity gap. The evaluation convention selects the highest available LF resolution, producing the 64²→128² task — but the crystalline fields are band-limited below the coarse grid's Nyquist frequency, so the exact per-row copy error is ≤ 1.66e-6 on all 100 test rows. The certified reference error 0.018257 was ~100% linear-interpolation error from the fixed lifting operation. The real fidelity gap occurs at 32²→128², which the convention never evaluated. Resolution: ADR r3-0005, ratified today — the task moves to the coarse rung with an exact spectral reference; the repaired task is honest but low-resolution (minimum detectable change 65.8%, disclosed on every future claim).
  • Checkpoint resumption silently invalidated a baseline re-evaluation campaign. Models being re-evaluated on repaired data resumed already-completed checkpoints (records show resumed_from_step = 5000, training 1.3–2.9 s), so pre-repair weights were evaluated on post-repair data — and every existing consistency check passed, because references recompute live while only the weights were stale. All of round 3's own experiment runs were clean (0/225 affected). 32 runs were quarantined and retrained; a permanent stale-checkpoint check now runs inside every baseline build. Fresh training improved every contaminated baseline, so the contamination had made experimental comparisons look better than they were.
  • The audit experiment measured the audit tools' own accuracy. The train_seconds staleness heuristic found 0 unique true positives against 62 false alarms across 859 runs and is being deleted. File modification times cannot establish data identity (the repair copy preserved them); only a content hash stored in the checkpoint can distinguish a training-invalidating data change from a harmless test-split trim. Building that mechanism is the batch-2 audit experiment.
  • Tolerance setting now rests on a measured numerical-precision limit. One audit condition triggered at 1.106e-9 against a 1e-9 tolerance — ~70× below the variation introduced by the float64→float32 data-loading boundary. Per-dataset analysis shows suitable tolerances range from 2e-9 to 0.31 (sod_1d, where an exact-equality std == 0 check amplifies one unit-in-the-last-place by 8.4e6×; confined to a non-evaluated guard dataset, no released result is wrong).
  • A minimum detectable difference of zero means unobservable, not zero. For both ifc datasets this round cannot observe uncertainty in the reference itself, understating absolute thresholds ~2.15×; no current claim changes.

4 · How the harness itself is performing

  • Batch 1 completed all four experiment lines at 3 seeds in ~5 days (~2 of them the repair); batch 2 went from literature search through preregistration, implementation, adversarial review, and first cluster jobs in under a day.
  • Preregistration and minimum-effect thresholds prevented two false headlines (the −10.1% LF-training "win" and the condition-dimension explanation), and adversarial reviews caught real defects before submission: one falsification condition used the wrong statistic; one admission rule differed measurably from its preregistered description and was adjudicated before any result existed.
  • Certified and retracted results stay traceable: only results backed by the audit certification are called certified; every retraction is annotated in place with the historical numbers preserved; the 0-for-7 novelty record is reported as such.
  • The state-file design survived an orchestrator failure: the launch session died on 2026-08-08; a fresh session resumed losslessly from the state files, executed the repair under standing delegation while holding the stop-the-line decision for the operator, and has run the round since.

6 · Batch-2 midpoint (added 2026-08-10, evening)

  • COMPLETE Factorised head (r3s1-B2) — falsified its novelty claim; the stream recommends its own consolidation. The statistically calibrated shape-selection rule was pre-registered head-to-head against the plain "raise the cap" bug-fix, and the plain fix wins. What survives: a confirmed fisher_kpp improvement of 13.35 (1.30× that dataset's minimum claimable effect), attributable entirely to the cap fix; and the input-space-expansion repair is now a certified null — worse than inert (3.62× the threshold), because widening the correction gate's input collapses it (19 columns of pure noise collapse it identically). The stream-closing measurement: on fisher_kpp, even a perfect predictor restricted to the model's 50 principal shapes (oracle ceiling 298.83) still loses to the 6-parameter affine reference (270.54) — the basis, not the selection rule, is the binding constraint there; cahn_hilliard retains 26.8× of real headroom.
  • COMPLETE IC-stack (r3s2-B2) — falsified on exactly one clause (an instrument-acceptance leg), while the route comparison itself came out affirmative. The stack beats the matched direct route at panel level by 1.62 (3.2× the minimum claimable effect, sign-stable at every seed), though no single-dataset win is claimable (both ifc datasets still lose the mandatory affine reference). The clause that fired is honest instrument accounting: with only 3 fitting samples, cross-validation chose zero regularisation in the frequency band that matters on ifc_poisson, so the repair was inert exactly where batch 1 blew up. The replication arm reproduced batch 1's numbers within noise on every seed including the seed-2 blow-up, down to the identical amplification factor (66.219…) — proving the repaired arms removed it via the band-limit, not re-implementation drift. The stream's reference-to-beat improves from 12.96 to 10.09, recorded with the caveat that the card setting it is falsified on its own pre-registration.
  • RUNNING Value-of-coarse-data (r3s3-B2) — the cost-curve experiment (150 training runs across a ladder of coarse-data budgets) is on the cluster.
  • RUNNING Audit (r3s4-B2) — the checkpoint-binding instrument passed its labelled-defect exam exactly (every pre-registered detection count reproduced; zero false "retrain" verdicts), and its fairness probe fired as pre-registered: fitting reference baselines on 400 rows while scored models fit on 320 shifts cahn_hilliard's reference by 1.93× the minimum claimable effect. Per the clause this is escalated to the operator rather than silently absorbed; the proposed fix is a closed-form re-pricing of the affected batch-1 comparisons once the pricing stage lands (no re-runs needed).
  • DECIDED Scoring change (ADR r3-0006, operator decision). The headline score's denominator moves from "copy the coarse solve and enlarge it" to the best learned baseline, mf_fno_transfer_film. Because both scores are ratios to the same model RMSE, the switch is an exact per-dataset conversion: nothing is re-scored, the frozen evaluation layer is untouched, rankings within the panel are preserved, and in-flight batch-2 verdicts evaluate in the units their predictions were registered in. The recorded trade-off: the denominator becomes a trained, seed-dependent object, so its own 3-seed variance is certified and disclosed beside every converted number.
