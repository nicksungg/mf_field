# b30 pilot2500 — epoch-ladder pilot decision record

Date: 2026-08-14.
Operator: Eloise ("actually do ur rec" → option A, pilot ladder first, after being offered A pilot / B full / C none).

## Question

The benchmark_30 headline (film-relative skill geomean 0.6004; film transfer the better generalist) is a **200-epoch** verdict.
Film transfer is warm-started while r3s2_route trains from scratch per dataset, so training budget is the strongest open confound.
The campaign recorded no per-epoch validation, so plateau status at e200 is unknowable from existing artifacts.
This pilot measures the epoch dose-response before any full 29×3×2500 rerun (~145 GPU-h) is spent.

## Design

- Families: `r3s2_route_b30` (vendored, byte-identical, frozen) vs `mf_fno_transfer_film` (live repo dir, unchanged since f63bcbf 2026-08-01, same code as the campaign; cache `code_hash` guards drift).
- Epoch ladder {200, 600, 2500}, seed 0 only. The e200 rung reuses the closed campaign's seed-0 results (rev-eac7b48e); e600/e2500 are fresh runs.
  Cosine LR annealing (`T_max=epochs`) makes each rung its own regime — rungs are independent runs, never warm-started from a lower rung.
  `ckpt_dir` is keyed `ckpt_{ds}_e{epochs}_s{seed}`, so rungs cannot cross-contaminate and preemption resume stays safe.
- 8 pilot datasets (from the campaign leaderboard, mean-over-seeds film/r3s2 ratio):
  wins to test for persistence — sharp__fisher_kpp_2d (6.42), ext__helmholtz_2d (5.16), sharp__allen_cahn_2d (2.63);
  narrow film wins to test for flips — poisson_local (0.935), sharp__euler (0.924), darcy_generated (0.821);
  structural film wins to test whether budget explains them — ifc_heat (0.234, round-3 home turf), poisson_generated (0.123, worst blowout).
- Isolation: all pilot artifacts under `mffp_autoresearch_outputs/benchmark30/pilot2500/` (own results/cache/logs/diag roots via `ROUND2_EVAL_RESULTS`/`ROUND2_EVAL_CACHE`); the closed campaign tree is only read (e200 scores), never written.
- Scripts are generated by `make_pilot_sbatch.py` as mechanical transforms of the certified rev-eac7b48e seed-0 scripts (path swap, epochs swap, dataset subset, job name/time); the frozen `--env` recipe blocks are untouched.
- Jobs (partition gpu, H200, mail END,FAIL): r3s2-e600 (8 ds, 10 h cap), r3s2-e2500-a/-b (4+4 ds, 16 h cap each), film-e600 (8 ds, 6 h cap), film-e2500 (8 ds, 16 h cap).
  Estimated actual usage ~15 GPU-h total (scaled from e200 per-dataset wall times).
  A CPU aggregation job (`aggregate_ladder.py`, afterany on all five) writes `results/ladder_table.{md,csv}` when the ladder completes.

## Decision rule (declared before results)

- If the film/r3s2 ratio ladder is flat (each dataset's e2500 ratio within noise of e200), the 200-epoch verdict stands; no full campaign; the ~130 GPU-h delta is saved.
- If ratios move materially toward r3s2 (narrow losses flip, or wins grow), escalate to the full 29-dataset × 3-seed × 2500 campaign for a citable number.
- Single-seed pilot results are trend reads only — round-3 cross-node drift means no headline claims from this pilot.
- The pilot set is enriched for r3s2 wins/near-wins by design; its geomean is not comparable to the benchmark_30 headline.
