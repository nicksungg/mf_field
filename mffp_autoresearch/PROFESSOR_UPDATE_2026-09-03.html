01

What round 4 is testing, and how

The goal for the whole round is a model that beats your film row in final_error_matrix.csv across benchmark_30, trained at 2500 epochs per stage. That row is the bar, fixed in writing before any challenger existed, together with the rules for judging a margin against it: below 5% is not credible, 5–10% is suggestive, above 10% is credible, and a per-dataset ratio under 1.5× is descriptive rather than a win.

We deliberately did not train our own film at 2500 epochs to serve as the bar. The point of the round is to beat a number you produced independently of our harness.

Every challenger runs the same sequence. A design document states the mechanism and its falsification thresholds, and those thresholds ship as executable functions with tests rather than as prose, so they cannot be softened later by rewording. A pilot at 200 epochs on a pre-signed 14-dataset development subset costs under an hour of GPU time and kills most ideas. Only a passing pilot earns certification: all 30 datasets, 2500 epochs per stage, three seeds, roughly 65 H100-hours.

Inference is condition-only throughout — the high-fidelity field is predicted from the small condition vector alone. Re-running a coarse solve at test time stays banned.

02

The five challengers, and what each one ruled out

C1

Film plus three zero-initialised input columns: nearest-neighbour retrieval, retrieval mean, rendered initial condition killed at pilot

Two of three falsification clauses failed. The retrieval columns actively hurt — 19.8% worse than film overall, worst case 8× on ext__wave_2d — and a derangement control (shuffle the conditions, re-run) confirmed they were exploiting almost no information. The initial-condition column was the one part that worked, at +82% on sharp__fisher_kpp_2d. That signal is what became C3a.

C2

Tail-averaging of film's weights over the last quarter of training, plus spectral weight decay no-go at 2500

The premise was that film drifts late in training. At 200 epochs the averaging window was inert (0.56% worse). At 2500 the within-run drift is essentially nil on the sharp datasets — at most 1.023 on sharp__allen_cahn_2d — so the average is the last iterate. Useful negative: the “film is worse at 2500 on sharp problems” pattern we had seen across separate runs is not within-run drift.

C3a

Film plus one zero-initialised channel carrying the closed-form initial condition certified · beats the bar

The design identified six candidate datasets; in the certified runs the channel is live on four — sharp__allen_cahn_2d, sharp__cahn_hilliard, sharp__fisher_kpp_2d, sharp__phase_field_crystal_2d. On the other 26 the channel is identically zero and the network is film bit-for-bit, verified as bit-equality rather than asserted, which is what bounds the downside by construction. Section 3.

C3b

Zero-initialised low-rank, condition-driven multiplicative modulation of film's spectral weights failed certification

At 200 epochs it looked good — 5.9% better than film, 1.92× on lid_driven_cavity_generated — and the derangement control confirmed the win was real rather than an artefact. At 2500 epochs it lands 5.8% worse than the bar over 30 datasets. The pilot-tier gain did not survive the full budget, which is exactly the failure mode the two-tier protocol exists to catch.

C4

Three follow-ons: a prediction ensemble of C3a's own checkpoints, a capacity sweep, and the two C3 mechanisms combined in flight

The ensemble adds no training at all and is currently the round's largest effect. The capacity sweep separates “the mechanism helps” from “the model was too small”. Section 6.

04

The metric audit — what went wrong on our side

This is the part most relevant to anyone else comparing against your matrix, so it is worth stating carefully.

Retracted 2026-08-26

For about a week we believed your film was a systematically stronger lineage than ours — 2.51× better on ext__cahn_hilliard_2d, 3.19× on sharp__shallow_water_1d — which would have meant that no challenger built on our film could reach your panel regardless of merit. That conclusion was wrong, and it was wrong for a reason entirely on our side.

Our harness reports relative L2 in two aggregations: the mean over test samples of the per-sample relative error, and a ratio of summed norms over the whole split. On datasets with skewed per-sample errors the two differ by a factor of two or more. Your matrix is the per-sample mean; the pre-registered comparison clauses were reading the ratio-of-sums field. The two “lineages” were two columns of one lineage.

The proof chain

  • Our round-4 film and the factory's film agree to 1e-6 on a fixed input — same weights, same code.
  • The earlier training-ladder film numbers equal the round-4 runs' per-sample-mean values to printed precision on 8 of 8 overlapping datasets.
  • Panel geometric mean of your matrix over our film: 0.944 under the per-sample mean, against 0.729 under the ratio-of-sums.
  • The 2.51× apparent gap on ext__cahn_hilliard_2d becomes 1.003.

What changed as a result. The pre-registered FAIL verdicts still stand — C3b is worse than the bar under the corrected metric too, and its verdict was recorded before the audit. C3a moved from “loses to the bar” to the numbers in section 3. We added a metric contract that every comparison must resolve against, a gate that refuses to score a panel unless our own film reproduces your matrix within tolerance across the full 30, and explicit waived-unexplained semantics so that a dataset cannot be dropped from a panel without a written reason. That full-30 gate currently reports FAIL honestly, because 8 optional cells have not been run; it is not marked green on partial evidence.

06

Diagnostics about the benchmark, independent of any model

These came out of the round's Phase 0 and describe the benchmark rather than a challenger.

  • The error is shape, not level. At both budgets, the share of squared error attributable to a constant offset is small — at most 0.094 on 9 of the 10 datasets probed at 200 epochs, and 0.016–0.237 at 2500. A mean or level correction is not a lever.
  • There are at least three distinct spatial failure modes, not one. Interface-dominated (lid_driven_cavity_generated, where 0.898 of the error mass sits on 5% of the pixels), bimodal (sharp__fisher_kpp_2d), and bulk (sharp__allen_cahn_2d, sharp__cahn_hilliard, peaking well away from any interface). ifc_heat is the counter-example that kills a single-mode story: 0.086 of its error at the interface and 0.408 in the farthest bin. Any mechanism aimed only at sharpening interfaces leaves a large part of the panel untouched.
  • Twelve and a half times the training budget does not relocate the failure. On sharp__allen_cahn_2d the spatial error profile at 2500 epochs is effectively identical to the one at 200, while the total error is worse at the larger budget.
  • A zero-cost reference beats a trained model on several datasets — but far less than it first appeared. Simply resampling the coarse solution beats film at 2500 epochs on seven datasets, by up to 242× on sharp__allen_cahn_2d. That reference uses the test split's low-fidelity field, which no model may see — the scorer raises if it is exposed. The implementable version of the observation is worth 2 datasets and a 1.054× panel gain, not 2.072×. We recorded that correction against our own earlier draft, which had the larger number as its headline.
  • Numbers move across hardware more than expected. Evaluating the same certified checkpoints on CPU versus H100 differs by up to 148% on lid_driven_cavity_generated and 41% on heat_local, traced to TF32 defaults in cuDNN convolutions with no pin in the model family. Any comparison between numbers produced on different machines needs the device stated. Every certification number here is H100.
07

What is running now

All queued on a congested GPU partition as of this morning — 24 or more H100 and 45 or more H200 jobs pending ahead of ours.

C4-ens — ensembling C3a's own three checkpoints, no additional training

The CPU run is complete: the ensemble is better than the mean of its own three seeds on 30 of 30 datasets, geometric mean 0.816 — 18% lower error — with the largest gains on the smooth and elliptic problems and the smallest on the sharp ones.

Combining that ratio with the H100 three-seed means, so the cross-device offset cancels, gives a device-consistent estimate of +26.8% over the bar on 30 datasets and +31.2% on 28, with six per-dataset clears above 1.5× (sharp__fisher_kpp_2d 6.75×, sharp__helmholtz_2d 1.74×, sharp__burgers_1d 1.70×, poisson_generated 1.64×, sharp__allen_cahn_2d 1.57×, heat_generated 1.53×) and 5 of 30 still below the bar.

This is an estimate until the GPU evaluation runs, and it carries three times the training compute of a single-seed bar, labelled as such wherever it appears.

C4b, a capacity sweep on C3a's trunk in three configurations, has its pilot queued; it separates “the mechanism helps” from “the model was under-parameterised”. C4a, C3a's initial-condition channel combined with C3b's condition modulation, is mid-build with its specification and plan converged and seven implementation tasks committed. The seed-42 film probe on lid_driven_cavity_generated is queued on three device classes for section 5.

The judgement call ahead is whether the ensemble result, if it survives GPU evaluation, becomes the round's headline. A 27% panel improvement at zero extra training cost is a larger effect than any architecture change the round has produced — and it says something about the benchmark's variance rather than about the model, which is how we would report it.
