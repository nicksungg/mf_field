## Proposal reasoning

### Step 0 — B3 or close?

Close is legitimate (program.md 4.6 via r1 4.6; B2 part 7 poses it). I weighed it seriously and rejected it on four grounds, each checkable:

1. **Two headline findings of this stream are currently post-hoc.** The decorrelation law and the sign reversal on allen_cahn were both *fitted to and measured on B2's own shipped folds* (part 6 T3-F1/T3-F4). The round report's instrument-defect section would carry them as curve fits. One 17-minute run converts them into a pre-registered out-of-sample test. That is a qualitative upgrade of the round's strongest non-obvious result, not an incremental number.
2. **The `preds_test.npz` build gate is otherwise permanently unlifted.** B2 part 7 states the cahn_hilliard residual (8.55x mce) "is not merely open, it is currently UNTESTABLE from the stream's artifacts". Closing bakes that into the report. A card that ships per-arm predictions makes the one honest deficit on this panel testable — and, per B2 part 7 item 4, every future projection/decorrelation probe too.
3. **The cost is small and measured, not guessed.** B2's full panel+guard job was 16.83 min on an H200 (part 5 `wall_clock_detail`, job 66189580, 9.4% of a 3 h budget), and B3 *removes* work (drops `decoder_small` and the 8-point rank sweep) while adding only CPU-cheap closed-form fits. One decoder x 6 datasets x 200 epochs.
4. **Criterion accounting does not argue for close.** Criterion 1 runs through r2s3-B3/r2s4 and B3 does not compete for that budget; criterion 2 (skill < 1) is out of reach for every arm in this regime, so no card in this stream can be justified by it — which means the stream's remaining value is exactly the measurement/instrument value B3 delivers.

Decision: **run B3**, shaped as an instrument-repair-and-re-price card, not a capacity swing.

### Step 1 — which verdict row to claim

- C2 is named by the websearcher as "the strongest available contribution shape" and "best-supported novel composition for B3". Its mechanism is preempted (Bates & Granger 1969) and will be named as such on the card; what is open is the *benchmark-protocol reading*, which no fetched source treats.
- C1 supplies the scored-arm form (per-mode OOF map bank). It is `preempted-but-MF-composition-open`, and the card must declare one-regressor-per-mode as presumed prior art and ASAMS as prior art for automatic selection. C1 is *not* the headline; it is the control the decoder must beat.
- C3 is `preempted (cite)` -> enters only as a bug fix in the recipe (SET indexing + form-matched basis), never as a contribution.
- C4 is unchecked -> diagnostic only, no falsification weight.

So: **headline C2, scored arm from C1, C3 as recipe-level bug fix, C4 as a reported diagnostic.**

### Step 2 — the load-bearing design move

Every arm this stream has ever scored passed through a shared post-hoc stage (Wiener gains + floor blend). B2 proved the stage decides comparisons by decorrelation. The repair is not a better stage — it is to **make the scored column stage-free** and demote the stage to a measured object:

- `test_hf` = the closed-form head with **no Wiener, no blend**.
- The stage's outputs become `stage_*` reference splits (never prefixed `test`), reported beside the raw column.
- Before any test tensor is touched, the family writes a **rho-prelude JSON** from calibration-fold-only moments (sigma_arm, sigma_base, rho per arm x base) plus the Bates-Granger two-forecast prediction of each arm's post-blend test error. Realized post-stage numbers are compared to that prediction afterwards.

This is the whole C2 claim executed prospectively, and it is what makes L2 an out-of-sample test rather than a re-fit.

### Step 3 — the scored arm (C1 + C3 repairs)

Direction bank = {DC / spatial-mean direction} union {POD modes 0..49 of the **DC-removed** fit-fold residual, in the **selected centering form**}. Per direction: 5-fold OOF R^2 from the condition under a map bank {affine, quadratic, RBF kernel ridge, k-NN}; the best family per direction is selected out of fold; the fitted **SET** = directions clearing tau = 0.1 (cardinality capped at 32, floored at 1).

Three B2 defects die here at once: (a) **SET indexing** replaces window indexing (worth 5.62x mce on cahn_hilliard); (b) the basis is **form-matched** to the selected centering (kills the add/fact index-transfer defect, T1-F7); (c) including the DC direction as an explicit candidate makes `ref_dc_only` a *selected special case* of the head instead of a competitor picked on test — which is what makes the "closed form beats the decoder" statement legitimate on pfc/fisher_kpp, where the SET after DC removal is empty (0 of 30 identifiable, T2-F5).

### Step 4 — alternatives weighed and rejected

- **A bigger/other decoder (DeepONet branch-trunk, SIREN/INR).** Rejected: B1 falsified capacity; B2 showed the surviving cell is 20.6%-basis-energy modes at OOF R^2 <= 0.035, i.e. an identification limit, and program.md 12.1 records bare conditioned decoders as `preempted`. A new decoder family would spend the round's last batch re-testing a settled question with an unrepaired instrument.
- **Widening the Wiener grid past 2.0** (the naive reading of the pinned band-3 gain). Explicitly rejected by B2 part 7 item 3 with numbers: cosine 0.1218, LS-optimal 0.6401 (a shrink), ORACLE 0.70; widening makes the cell strictly worse. B3 caps the grid at [0, 1.5] and adds an explicit stand-down (identity) candidate.
- **Keeping the staged scored column and merely adding a raw reference column.** Rejected: the scored column is what enters part 5, the anchor comparison and the round report; leaving it staged keeps the artifact in the headline number. Reversing the roles costs nothing and is the actual protocol claim.
- **A pure diagnostic card (no training).** Rejected: the rho-law test needs a *fresh* arm set with a genuinely different rho (a trained decoder at rho ~0.7 vs a closed form at rho ~1.0); without a trained arm there is only one rho class and the law cannot be tested out of sample. Also fails the `preds_test.npz` gate, which by construction requires decoder predictions.
- **Scoring only the 3 live cells.** Rejected as a *run* decision (panel is immutable, program.md 5.2 — all 6 must be scored and tabled) but adopted as a *falsification* decision: the legs live on allen_cahn / cahn_hilliard / (pfc for the law), helmholtz is report-only, fisher_kpp is excluded from the law leg because its mce 0.00071 is below any estimator's resolution, ifc_poisson is anecdote-grade (calib fold = all of N = 5).
- **A panel-geomean leg.** Rejected: B1 item 4 (a leg decided inside the mce band) and B2's precedent. The card pre-registers none, and states in advance that the stage-free geomean will likely be *worse* than B2's staged 18.36 — because the hedge buys score, not accuracy attribution. Saying that before the run is part of the claim.

## Proposal

- **Category**: gap / instrument-repair and stage-free re-pricing — Bates-Granger decorrelation accounting as an evaluation-protocol defect detector, with a per-mode out-of-fold-selected closed-form head as the standing control.
- **Card type**: `model`
- **Motivation**: prior-art verdict C2, verbatim: **`preempted-but-MF-composition-open (cite)` — best-supported novel composition for B3**; "The **mechanism is preempted and must be named as Bates-Granger combination in the card.** Open: its use as an **evaluation-protocol defect detector** — appending a shared floor-blend stage to every arm of a benchmark converts an architecture comparison into a decorrelation comparison (arm class that IS the base at rho = 1.0000 unpayable; decisive cell reverses by 2.07-7.15x mce; lambda <= 0.25 => reporting the base)." The scored arm's form comes from verdict C1, verbatim: **`preempted-but-MF-composition-open (cite)`**, "One-regressor-per-mode is **presumed prior art (do not claim)**... Open after 3 targeted refutation searches with zero named sources: per-mode **family** selection by OOF R^2, used as the standing ~10^1-10^2-parameter control arm a 10^7-parameter decoder must beat on a **copy-LF-skill panel under the stripped no-LF-at-test view**, with the per-mode ORACLE ceiling reported." C3 enters as a declared bug fix only (`preempted (cite)`, "Ship as a bug fix / instrument, never as a contribution"); C4 as a diagnostic only ("NOT PRIOR-ART-CHECKED THIS LOOP... must not be headlined as novel").
- **Concrete config**: as Steps 2-3 above. New from-scratch family `models_r2/r2s1_stagefree_permode` on the round2-substrate; condition-only forward signature; stripped view; leakage tripwire; no LF at any stage. Disjoint fit 80% / model-selection 10% / calibration 10% folds, asserted disjoint, LOO + fixed-epoch when N_train < 20 (ifc_poisson). Scored arm `test_hf` = stage-free per-mode SET head. Reference splits: `ref_head_set_affine` (affine-only, isolates the map bank), `ref_head_window_affine` (B2's rule, isolates the arity repair), `ref_decoder_big` (FiLM-FNO w64/b4/modes16/latent16, 200 epochs, early-stopped on the model fold, RAW), `stage_wiener_{head,decoder}`, `stage_blend_{head,decoder}` (decorrelation-audited base set), `stage_bg_{head,decoder}` (Bates-Granger minimum-variance weights from the calibration-fold prediction-error covariance, Ledoit-Wolf shrunk), floors `ref_dc_only`, `ref_zero`, `ref_train_mean`, `ref_nn_condition` (last three seam-checked to `state/anchors/floors.json` at 1e-9, RAISING on mismatch). Guards at contract tier. **Build gates**: (G-A) every decoder arm ships `preds_test.npz` per dataset; (G-B) the rho-prelude JSON is written from calibration-fold-only quantities before any test tensor is read; (G-C) floor seam check raises; (G-D) Wiener grid capped at [0, 1.5] with an explicit stand-down candidate and the LS-optimal per-band gain reported; (G-E) any blend base with calib-fold |rho| > 0.95 to an arm's error is DROPPED for that arm and the drop recorded. Diagnostics (JSON, non-scored): per-direction OOF R^2 x map-family table with selected family and the ORACLE ceiling labelled ORACLE; SET-vs-window index sets; rho matrix and predicted-vs-realized stage payoff; Wiener gains vs LS-optimal gains; H2 projection of the decoder's own test predictions onto the fit-fold basis, split into fitted-SET / in-window-complement / basis-orthogonal energy (cahn_hilliard, allen_cahn) — diagnostic only.
- **Recipe**: see `report.md` (complete JSON, transcribed verbatim by the starter).
- **Expected outcome**: table in Step 5. Primary movable metric: the per-dataset skill of `test_hf` against `ref_decoder_big`, in the RAW frame. allen_cahn predicted margin ~50 skill units = ~57x the certified mce 0.87970; cahn_hilliard predicted residual ~0.65 = ~7x mce 0.09125; arity repair ~0.45 = ~5x mce. Panel geomean ~19.1 vs anchor 23.0636, reported with no falsification weight.
